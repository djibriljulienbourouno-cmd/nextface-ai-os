# NextFace AI OS Product Spec

## Vision

NextFace AI OS is an independent AI workspace for models and creators. It helps users improve model readiness, personal presentation, portfolio quality, casting confidence, style, grooming, and career organization.

## Core workspace

The app contains:
- AI Chat
- Conversations
- Projects
- Library
- Tools
- Deep Research
- Reports
- Admin Dashboard

## Research-backed AI principle

The app must research online when answers depend on current facts. Every research-based response should show citations, confidence, last checked date, source quality, warnings, and next actions.

## Visual AI Coach

Includes visual guide modules for:
- posture
- posing
- facial expression
- runway walk
- grooming routines
- photo angles
- general wellness habits
- movement demonstrations

All wellness content includes health disclaimers.

## Style & Skin Quality Advisor

Users can upload outfits/photos and receive:
- clothing recommendations
- style direction
- color palette guidance
- grooming advice
- skin-quality routine suggestions
- skincare routine guidance
- product-category suggestions
- photo-ready preparation

Not medical advice. Users should consult a dermatologist/professional for persistent skin issues. Product recommendations require current source checks.

## Ethical retention

Retention is based on real progress:
- Model Journey levels
- progress streaks
- weekly challenges
- achievement badges
- before/after score tracking
- monthly reports
- personalized next actions
- reminders
- saved goals
- client milestones
- portfolio tracker
- non-manipulative gamification

No shame mechanics, dark patterns, fake urgency, or addictive manipulation.

## AI App Builder

Separate affordable one-time premium service for users who want a simple AI app, portfolio site, landing page, or creator tool.
