# Online Research & Evidence System

## Goal

Every answer that depends on current or factual information must be researched online and justified with sources.

## When research is required

Use live internet research when the user asks about:
- agencies
- castings
- prices
- stores
- products
- current trends
- locations
- opening hours
- competitions
- legal/financial/medical-adjacent facts
- skincare products or product availability
- platform rules
- deadlines
- anything likely to change

## Answer requirements

Every research-backed answer must include:
- clear answer
- reasoning summary
- citations/source cards
- source quality
- confidence level
- last checked date
- warnings if information is unverified or outdated
- next actions

## Source quality categories

- official source
- trusted media
- commercial source
- social source
- low-confidence source

## Deep Research workflow

1. Query planner
2. Web research checklist
3. Source comparison table
4. Verified answer
5. Citations
6. Final recommendation
7. Next actions

## Safety rules

Never make unsupported claims for:
- agencies
- castings
- prices
- products
- skincare/medical advice
- legal advice
- financial advice

Skincare and wellness answers must include a disclaimer and recommend a dermatologist/professional for persistent issues.
